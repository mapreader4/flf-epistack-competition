B.2  Map of HSM, west half

I have only found one map of HSM which labels the individual stalls; I have included the
west half in figure 12.
Shown in figure 12 is the west half of the HSM market. ⟦1⟧ ⟦2⟧ The market is organized into streets or
alleys which are numbered 1 to 15; ⟦3⟧ each alley has many individual shops. Here the numbers
are given on a regular grid, but in the WHO report ⟦4⟧ ⟦5⟧ ⟦6⟧ some shops span multiple grid cells, and
thus presumably have multiple numbers. ⟦7⟧ ⟦8⟧ ⟦9⟧ I have used the locations of cases as marked on the
WHO map and compared those locations with this map to identify the stall numbers of those
cases; ⟦10⟧ ⟦11⟧ ⟦12⟧ the results were in quite good agreement with other sources that reported early cases
by stall numbers. ⟦13⟧ ⟦14⟧ ⟦15⟧ ⟦16⟧ ⟦17⟧ ⟦18⟧ I think this map is erroneous in prefixing an 'X' on stall numbers in alley
13. ⟦19⟧ ⟦20⟧ ⟦21⟧ ⟦22⟧ ⟦23⟧ ⟦24⟧ I think this map is not to scale. ⟦25⟧ ⟦26⟧ Orange-red shading are locations with environmental
samples positive for covid; ⟦27⟧ ⟦28⟧ sky blue for those with environmental samples which were all
negative. ⟦29⟧ ⟦30⟧ ⟦31⟧ Unshaded locations had no environmental samples. ⟦32⟧ This is taken from figure S1B
of [12].
