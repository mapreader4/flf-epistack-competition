⟦1⟧ ⟦2⟧ ⟦3⟧ Rootclaim identified the possibility that the mahjong room in HSM may have been a nexus

observations linking early covid cases to the mahjong room; and two, ⟦4⟧ ⟦5⟧ ⟦6⟧ a supposition that the
mahjong room would have an unusually high rate of disease transmission.
I think neither facet is sufficiently substantiated. ⟦7⟧ ⟦8⟧ ⟦9⟧ ⟦10⟧ ⟦11⟧ Rootclaim claims that at least 35 early
covid cases have been linked to the mahjong room or mahjong players in some way ([1]

anecdotes. ⟦12⟧ ⟦13⟧ 27 of these come from a single source, ⟦14⟧ a Chinese-language video that I cannot

against the background rate of mahjong players.
