of presenting my best guess as to ⟦1⟧ the origin of the covid pandemic. ⟦2⟧ I will present this in
the structure of a Bayesian inference computation, though not wholly quantitative, ⟦3⟧ ⟦4⟧ ⟦5⟧ with my
choice of numbers being subjective and uncertain.
⟦6⟧ ⟦7⟧ LetZ be the hypothesis that covid emerged from a zoonotic spillover in HSM, and ⟦8⟧ LL that
covid resulted from gain-of-function research in WIV; ⟦9⟧ ⟦10⟧ ⟦11⟧ ⟦12⟧ these are very slightly narrowings from

⟦20⟧ ⟦21⟧ The first step is the most challenging, ⟦22⟧ to estimate the priors ⟦23⟧ P (Z) and ⟦24⟧ P (LL). ⟦25⟧ ⟦26⟧ These
