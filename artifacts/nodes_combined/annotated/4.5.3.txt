4.5.3  Empirical evidence of market outbreaks

It is a great challenge to argue from first principles that a certain environment is espe-
cially conducive to covid transmission. Recall how contentious even (comparatively) easily-
measured aspects like fomite transmission or size of respiratory particles were in 2020; and
now we hope to quantify the infectivity rate of being in one particular shopping center?
