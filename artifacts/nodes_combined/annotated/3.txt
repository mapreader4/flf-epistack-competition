(⟦52⟧ What's more, we can get qualitatively the same result using far fewer than 100 data points;

they are the ones that make the smallest contribution to the final result, and most will cancel
with each other. ⟦54⟧ Thus by making only 10 interesting observations we get more-or-less the
same result as if we had made 100, though with a bit more randomness.)
