7  Analysis

Having criticized others' arguments at great length, at last I come to the more difficult task
of presenting my best guess as to the origin of the covid pandemic. I will present this in
the structure of a Bayesian inference computation, though not wholly quantitative, with my
choice of numbers being subjective and uncertain.
⟦1⟧ LetZ be the hypothesis that covid emerged from a zoonotic spillover in HSM, and LL that
covid resulted from gain-of-function research in WIV; ⟦2⟧ these are very slightly narrowings from
the actual hypotheses at stake.
Note that ⟦3⟧ if you accept Rootclaim's argument in section 3.1, then the definition I gave
for Z is quite a significant narrowing, as they believe the observational evidence is equally
consistent with zoonotic spillover anywhere in Wuhan as in HSM, and so Z would have a
suitably greater prior (but much lower posterior).
⟦4⟧ The first step is the most challenging, to estimate the priors P (Z) and P (LL). These
numbers will be given as annual risk, ⟦5⟧ so can also be interpretted as the yearly rate at which
pandemics emerge from those sources.

Claims:
⟦1⟧ [ASSUMPTION] The covid pandemic originated from either a zoonotic spillover in HSM or gain-of-function research in WIV
⟦2⟧ [ASSUMPTION] The definition of Z as a zoonotic spillover in HSM is a narrowing of the actual hypothesis at stake
⟦3⟧ [ASSUMPTION] Rootclaim's argument in section 3.1 is a valid perspective on the observational evidence
⟦4⟧ [ASSUMPTION] The priors P(Z) and P(LL) can be estimated as annual risk
⟦5⟧ [ASSUMPTION] The yearly rate at which pandemics emerge from zoonotic spillover or gain-of-function research can be interpreted from the estimated priors
