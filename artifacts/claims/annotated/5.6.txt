5.6  Leading proline

⟦1⟧ The amino acids near the sars-cov-2 FCS are
QTQTNSPRRARSVAS
⟦2⟧ whereas the most closely related known virus banal-20-52 has
QTQTNSRSVAS
at this location. ⟦3⟧ The difference between these is PRRA.
⟦4⟧ The RRAR subsequence constitutes an FCS, which explains why a laboratory worker might
insert a sequence like RRA, but wherefore the proline P?
⟦5⟧ I see no satisfying explanation for what might motivate the decision to include a proline
at this location, ⟦6⟧ and it seems quite unlikely that the natural precursor virus happened to
have such an inserted proline at the exact location that researchers add an additional insert.
⟦7⟧ However we should not expect every decision someone makes to have an obvious post hoc
explanation. ⟦8⟧ There are many arbitrary choices during the course of a genetic engineering
experiment, and it is hardly strange if a few of them are not the most canonical or optimal.
⟦9⟧ I do not think the proline requires a specific explanation under the lab leak theory (and
likewise the "out-of-frame" nature of the insert).
