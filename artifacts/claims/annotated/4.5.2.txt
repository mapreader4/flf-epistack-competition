⟦2⟧ We get a reasonable chronology for January: 4.76 doublings in 23 days is 4.8 days doubling
time, which is definitely higher than the true value, but not bad for a simplistic model that
wasn't intended to be extrapolated to January. ⟦3⟧ In December we have 3 doublings in 21 days

⟦5⟧ Rootclaim's model looks good after the HSM closure but does not line up with observations
early in the market outbreak; it overestimates cases outside HSM and underestimates cases
within. ⟦6⟧ Assuming some randomness in the timing of the index cases and the ascertainment
rates and the doubling time, we can finangle the model numbers to be kind of consistent
with observations, but it clearly gets worse and worse the earlier in the outbreak one looks.

Claims:
⟦1⟧ The ascertainment rate used by Rootclaim is 10%.
⟦2⟧ The doubling time of the COVID-19 cases in January is approximately 4.8 days according to Rootclaim's model.
⟦3⟧ The doubling time of the COVID-19 cases in December is approximately 7 days according to Rootclaim's model.
⟦4⟧ The doubling time from the index non-HSM case to market closure is approximately 2.14 days.
⟦5⟧ Rootclaim's model overestimates cases outside HSM and underestimates cases within HSM early in the market outbreak.
⟦6⟧ Rootclaim's model can be made kind of consistent with observations by assuming some randomness in the timing of the index cases and the ascertainment rates and the doubling time.
