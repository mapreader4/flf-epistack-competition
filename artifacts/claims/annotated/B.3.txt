B.3  Vendor cases

⟦1⟧ Of the 55 HSM-linked cases in the WHO report, 53 were acquired at HSM and 2 acquired
indirectly; ⟦2⟧ 30 of these were vendors who work at fixed shops in HSM (annex page 177).
(Some of the other 23 were also HSM employees or regular visitors.) With much squinting
at the map on annex page 181, and comparing to figure S2 of [12], the locations of each of
the 30 vendor cases can be identified. ⟦3⟧ I have consolidated this with the HSM vendor cases
found by Dr Marion Koopmans from the data file of [12], located at https://github.com/
sars-cov-2-origins/huanan-market-environment/blob/main/metadata/cases.csv . ⟦4*⟧ (She
presumably had access to this data due to being on the 2021 WHO investigative team, specif-
ically as the head of the molecular epidemiology subteam.) Date ranges can be narrowed in
some cases by cross referencing information in other parts of the WHO report.
⟦5⟧ Note that the case data from Dr Koopmans is simply an unannotated csv file with no context
or discussion that I found in a github repository linked to from a paper she co-authored.
Thus the data has no implied warranty of validity - it could easily be some draft or working
data that was accidentally uploaded. ⟦6⟧ None-the-less it does match with case maps she has
made in public presentations.
