cially conducive to covid transmission. ⟦2*⟧ Recall how contentious even (comparatively) easily-

now we hope to quantify the infectivity rate of being in one particular shopping center?

This is not as bad for Rootclaim as it appears. ⟦30⟧ While the outbreak in the Xinfadi market
in Beijing did appear to start from frozen salmon brought to the market, ⟦31⟧ the Dalian and
Kashgar outbreaks only secondarily involved a market, so the market index case was human
to human transmission. Let us first look at Kashgar, in Figure 7.
⟦32⟧ In the Kashgar outbreak, 75 different infected individuals attended a large farm market event
that had approximately 20 thousand participants (out of a population of 700 thousand in
Kashgar); ⟦33⟧ this lead to 20 others at the market becoming infected. ⟦34⟧ Of those 20, 5 attended
a wedding, leading to 36 infections at that wedding; ⟦35⟧ this makes weddings appear to be a far
greater risk of spreading covid than markets. ⟦36⟧ Arguably the R0 at the market is less than 1.
⟦37⟧ This would be a relevant analog to the HSM outbreak if half a million people came to HSM
in one day, and more people brought the illness to it than from it.
