5.4.2  Proposed activities that would have involved WIV

⟦1⟧ The proposal also contains a detailed breakdown of the planned activities into 61 tasks, and
which groups will be doing each. Here I have summarized each step that WIV would have
been involved in:
⟦2⟧ ⟦3⟧ 1. Phase 1, tasks 3.1 and 3.2: Screen bat samples for sars-like viruses and sequence spike
proteins
⟦4⟧ 2. Task 6.1: make assays for identifying antibodies to known sars-like viruses
⟦5⟧ 3. Tasks 6.2 and 6.3: verify assays on rabbits and blood samples from people who had
SARS-1
⟦6⟧ 4. Task 6.4: test assays on people in Yunnan to look for sars-like spillover events
⟦7⟧ 5. Tasks 7.5 and 8.5: test viral 62 vector vaccine on captive bats
⟦8⟧ 6. Phase 2, tasks 5.1 through 5.4: Identify cave features; surveil bats; deploy vaccines or
immune boosters to caves; surveil bats
⟦9⟧ As far as I can see, no genetic engineering would be performed at WIV, ⟦10⟧ nor would any
chimeric viruses be brought there, except prototype vaccines which use a raccoonpox chimera.
⟦11⟧ ⟦12⟧ ⟦13⟧ ⟦14⟧ WIV was to be involved with samples collected from bats, tests on bats, tests on people who
live near bats, and experiments in bat caves.
