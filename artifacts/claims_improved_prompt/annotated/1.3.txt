1.3  Format of the debate

⟦1*⟧ Rootclaim was created to demonstrate the usefulness of a particular methodology for ana-
lyzing complicated questions, where there exists abundant lines of circumstantial evidence
both for and against any particular hypothesis. After systematically evaluating each piece
of evidence, they can be integrated into a gestalt that gives a final level of confidence we can
have in each hypothesis.
⟦2⟧ I refer to this technique as Bayesian reasoning or Bayesian computation , which will be
discussed in the next section; ⟦3⟧ however Rootclaim are far from the only ones to use Bayesian
reasoning, ⟦4⟧ and I believe they claim to distinguish themselves from others in the details of
how the evaluate the evidence. ⟦5⟧ In any case I agree with their critiques of other methodologies
(or, frequently, lack thereof) ⟦6⟧ and that most people who get the right answer do so more by
chance than not.
⟦7⟧ This debate is the first organized by Rootclaim, of hopefully multiple (on different topics,
to be clear!), ⟦8⟧ as a series of 3 spoken debate sessions totalling 20 hours [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6] ⟦9⟧ and
various written supplements [7, 8]. ⟦10⟧ I am one of two judges, deciding independently, ⟦11⟧ with the
outcome of the debate determined by the average of our decisions.
⟦12⟧ I assess the debate structure designed by Rootclaim to be very fair ⟦13⟧ and not create any
advantage for them, ⟦14⟧ and it was clear that Saar consciously acted in good faith throughout.
⟦15⟧ To me, the debate felt more like a collaboration of four people working together to seek the
truth than an adversarial contest; it is rare to see contentious issues worked out in such a
productive manner between people acting in good faith.
⟦16⟧ As I find that both parties argued in good faith ⟦17⟧ and there were no significant violations of (the
spirit of) the debate rules, ⟦18⟧ my evaluation is based solely on the arguments as presented at an
object level, or my best interpretation thereof. ⟦19⟧ Peter identified and criticized a diversity of
lab leak theories put forth by other parties, ⟦20⟧ but these had no bearing on Rootclaim's theory.
⟦21⟧ Statements were evaluated independently of the credibility of other parties who assert them.
⟦22⟧ I did not attempt to "keep score" of which side made more factual errors or changed their
argument more often; ⟦23⟧ each claim was given independent credulity. ⟦24⟧ Neither side was laden
with the burden of proof. While in other contexts it might be appropriate and fair to dismiss
arguments a priori for rhetorical reasons (eg, as a defense against techniques like sea lioning
or gish gollop), ⟦25⟧ here my sole concern is whose theory is best supported by the evidence.
⟦26⟧ Per the rules of the debate, I am to evaluate the relative likelihood of the two hypotheses
advanced: ⟦27*⟧ Rootclaim's claim that the sars-cov-2 outbreak was due to gain-of-function labo-
ratory research, ⟦28⟧ and Peter's challenge that it was the result of natural spillover from wildlife
without laboratory involvement. Only the information provided through the debate, debate
