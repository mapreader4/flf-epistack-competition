This passage is part of a long list of planned genetic experiments. They will look for cleavage
sites, including furin cleavage sites, in existing cells, and genetically modify some of them to
introduce "human-specific" cleavage sites. It is unclear to me whether furin is human-specific.
I understand this passage to be referring to the chimeric viruses with known backbone
(SHCO14 or WIV16). They also discuss adding cleavage sites to common, low risk sars-like
viruses. After this passage, it goes on to discuss N-linked glycosation.

Claims:
⟦1⟧ The proposal will screen samples for SARSr-CoV nucleic acid.
⟦2*⟧ Full-length genomes or S genes of all SARSr-CoVs will be high-throughput sequenced followed by genome walking.
⟦3⟧ The S gene will be analyzed for its ability to bind human ACE2 by Biocore or virus entry assay.
⟦4*⟧ Chimeric Novel SARSr-CoV QS will be commercially synthesized.
⟦5*⟧ The SARSr-CoV S glycoprotein genes will be designed for insertion into SHCO14 or WIV16 molecular clone backbones.
⟦6⟧ SHCO14 and WIV16 have 88% and 97% S-protein identity to epidemic SARS-Urbani61.
⟦7⟧ The backbones are BSL-3, not select agents or subject to P3CO.
⟦8⟧ The backbones use bat SARSr-CoV backbones which are exempt.
⟦9⟧ The backbones are pathogenic to hACE2 transgenic mice.
⟦10⟧ In vitro pseudovirus binding assays will be conducted using established techniques.
⟦11⟧ Live virus binding assays will be conducted for isolated strains at WIV.
⟦12⟧ The proposal will look for sars-like viruses, sequence their spike proteins, simulate how well those spike proteins bind to human ACE2 receptors, and put the genes for those spike proteins into known viruses.
⟦13⟧ The known viruses will be given to mice with human ACE2 receptors.
⟦14⟧ After evaluating the chimeric viruses, some of them will be verified by testing the original wild viruses the spikes came from at WIV.
⟦15⟧ All SARSr-CoV S gene sequences will be analyzed for appropriately conserved proteolytic cleavage sites in S2.
⟦16*⟧ The presence of potential furin cleavage sites will be analyzed in all SARSr-CoV S gene sequences.
⟦17⟧ SARSr-CoV S with mismatches in proteolytic cleavage sites can be activated by exogenous trypsin or cathepsin L.
⟦18*⟧ Appropriate human-specific cleavage sites will be introduced where clear mismatches occur.
⟦19⟧ The growth potential of SARSr-CoV S with introduced human-specific cleavage sites will be evaluated in Vero cells and HAE cultures.
⟦20⟧ Several proteolytic cleavage sites will be ablated in SARS-CoV based on pseudotyped particle studies.
⟦21⟧ The impact of select SARSr-CoV S changes on virus replication and pathogenesis will be evaluated.
⟦22⟧ Deep sequence data will be reviewed for low abundant high risk SARSr-CoV that encode functional proteolytic cleavage sites.
⟦23⟧ SARS-Urbani refers to a reference genome of sars-cov-1, collected from Carlo Urbani.
⟦24⟧ Carlo Urbani was a doctor and biologist who first realized SARS-1 was a new disease.
⟦25⟧ Carlo Urbani's warning to the WHO likely saved many lives.
⟦26⟧ Carlo Urbani died of SARS-1 at age 46.
