B.1  WHO cases by date and connection to HSM

Aggregate case data is presented as bar graphs found in the WHO report [32], annex pages
146, 152, and 179 (and others). In total the WHO identifies 174 known cases in 2019
December. For each date in December, I have listed the number of cases with that onset
date according to whether WHO says they have an established connection to HSM, with
those with connections broken down into vendors who have a fixed shop at HSM and visitors
who do not. ("Visitor" includes 23 people who became infected at HSM and 2 people who
became infected indirectly. "Unknown" presumably means that the patient was unavailable
for interview.)
