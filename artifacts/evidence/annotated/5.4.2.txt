5.4.2  Proposed activities that would have involved WIV

The proposal also contains a detailed breakdown of the planned activities into 61 tasks, and
which groups will be doing each. Here I have summarized each step that WIV would have
been involved in:
1. ⟦1⟧ Phase 1, tasks 3.1 and 3.2: Screen bat samples for sars-like viruses and sequence spike
proteins
2. ⟦2⟧ Task 6.1: make assays for identifying antibodies to known sars-like viruses
3. ⟦3⟧ Tasks 6.2 and 6.3: verify assays on rabbits and blood samples from people who had
SARS-1
4. ⟦4⟧ Task 6.4: test assays on people in Yunnan to look for sars-like spillover events
5. ⟦5⟧ Tasks 7.5 and 8.5: test viral 62 vector vaccine on captive bats
6. ⟦6⟧ Phase 2, tasks 5.1 through 5.4: Identify cave features; surveil bats; deploy vaccines or
immune boosters to caves; surveil bats
⟦7⟧ As far as I can see, no genetic engineering would be performed at WIV, ⟦8⟧ nor would any
chimeric viruses be brought there, except prototype vaccines which use a raccoonpox chimera.
⟦9⟧ WIV was to be involved with samples collected from bats, tests on bats, tests on people who
live near bats, and experiments in bat caves.
