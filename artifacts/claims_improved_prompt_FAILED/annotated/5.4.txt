5.4  Project DEFUSE

⟦1⟧ In 2018, the EcoHealth Alliance submitted a grant request to DARPA for $14 million for
"Project DEFUSE", ⟦2⟧ a 3.5 year project on bat coronaviruses ⟦3⟧ in collaboration with 5 other
groups (located at UNC, Duke-National University Singapore, WIV, USGS National Wildlife
Health Center, and Palo Alto Research Center) [14, 20]. ⟦4⟧ To my knowledge, the project was
not funded. The project involved quite a large number of goals and steps and can be
organized as follows:
⟦5⟧ 1. Collect bat coronaviruses and perform computer and lab testing on them
⟦6⟧ 2. Deploy vaccines and immune boosting agents to wild bats
⟦7⟧ 3. Write an app for soldiers to figure out how likely bats are to have dangerous diseases
(gotta appeal to DARPA somehow?)

Claims:
⟦1⟧ The EcoHealth Alliance submitted a grant request to DARPA for $14 million for Project DEFUSE in 2018.
⟦2⟧ Project DEFUSE was a 3.5 year project on bat coronaviruses in collaboration with 5 other groups.
⟦3⟧ The 5 other groups collaborating on Project DEFUSE were located at UNC, Duke-National University Singapore, WIV, USGS National Wildlife Health Center, and Palo Alto Research Center.
⟦4⟧ To the author's knowledge, Project DEFUSE was not funded.
⟦5⟧ Project DEFUSE involved collecting bat coronaviruses and performing computer and lab testing on them.
⟦6⟧ Project DEFUSE involved deploying vaccines and immune boosting agents to wild bats.
⟦7⟧ Project DEFUSE involved writing an app for soldiers to figure out how likely bats are to have dangerous diseases.
