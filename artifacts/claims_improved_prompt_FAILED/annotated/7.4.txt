7.4  Environmental samples in HSM

As discussed in section 4.6.1, ⟦1⟧ the distribution of positive sars-cov-2 samples within HSM
appears to generally point to the region where live animals were kept, or more specifically
point to shop 6:29 which had been identified as a potential pandemic spillover location.
⟦2⟧ Of the 15 shops which were sampled 10 or more times, 12 had no positives, ⟦3⟧ 3 had one positive,
⟦4⟧ and shop 6:29 had the other 5 positives. ⟦5⟧ Arguably, this observation has a Bayes factor of 15
in favor of Z, ⟦6⟧ as LL has no viable explanation for this coincidence. ⟦7⟧ While this observation
is certainly very post hoc, ⟦8⟧ more careful statistical analysis places 6:29 at the geographical
center of the sample positivity ratio, ⟦9⟧ and the positive drainage samples likewise implicate
alley 6.
⟦10⟧ However the HSM environmental samples will not contribute to my Bayesian calculation, as
will be explained.
