A.5  Early mutation rate

⟦1⟧ Naively one expects that the mutation rate of a virus would be greatest immediately after
jumping species. ⟦2⟧ In principle this could be used to distinguish a zoonotic origin from a virus
that had been pre-adapted to a human host in the lab.
⟦3*⟧ In the proposal for Project DEFUSE, viruses would be evaluated based on disease they
cause in "humanized mice", ⟦4⟧ ie mice with human ACE2 receptors [14]. ⟦5⟧ While the result of
this filtering process would be better adapted to humans than wild viruses, ⟦6⟧ the effect would
presumably be confined to the receptor binding domain of sars-cov-2; ⟦7⟧ the rest of the virus
would not be selected for being specialized for human hosts. ⟦8⟧ Therefore I do not expect the
early mutation rate to be useful in distinguishing the hypotheses without significant and
very technical investigation.
