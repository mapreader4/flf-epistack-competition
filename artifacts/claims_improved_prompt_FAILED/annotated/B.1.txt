Claims:
⟦1⟧ The WHO report presents aggregate case data as bar graphs on annex pages 146, 152, and 179, among others.
⟦2⟧ The WHO identifies 174 known cases in 2019 December.
⟦3⟧ For each date in December, the number of cases with that onset date is listed according to whether WHO says they have an established connection to HSM.
⟦4⟧ The cases with connections to HSM are broken down into vendors who have a fixed shop at HSM and visitors who do not.
⟦5⟧ The category 'Visitor' includes 23 people who became infected at HSM and 2 people who became infected indirectly.
⟦6⟧ The category 'Unknown' presumably means that the patient was unavailable for interview.
⟦7⟧ On December 8, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM.
⟦8⟧ On December 11, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM.
⟦9⟧ On December 12, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM and 2 cases with a non-HSM connection.
⟦10⟧ On December 13, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM.
⟦11⟧ On December 15, 2019, there were 3 cases with a vendor connection to HSM.
⟦12⟧ On December 16, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM.
⟦13⟧ On December 17, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM, 1 case with a visitor connection to HSM, 3 cases with a non-HSM connection, and 1 case with an unknown connection.
⟦14⟧ On December 18, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM and 4 cases with a non-HSM connection.
⟦15⟧ On December 19, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM and 1 case with a visitor connection to HSM.
⟦16⟧ On December 20, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 4 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 5 cases with a non-HSM connection.
⟦17⟧ On December 21, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM and 2 cases with a visitor connection to HSM.
⟦18⟧ On December 22, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM and 4 cases with a visitor connection to HSM.
⟦19⟧ On December 23, 2019, there were 6 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 1 case with a visitor connection to HSM, and 7 cases with a non-HSM connection.
⟦20⟧ On December 24, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 6 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 1 case with an unknown connection.
⟦21⟧ On December 25, 2019, there were 7 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 2 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 8 cases with a non-HSM connection.
⟦22⟧ On December 26, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 6 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 1 case with an unknown connection.
⟦23⟧ On December 27, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 2 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 12 cases with a non-HSM connection.
⟦24⟧ On December 28, 2019, there were 3 cases with a vendor connection to HSM and 11 cases with a visitor connection to HSM.
⟦25⟧ On December 29, 2019, there were 2 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 10 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 2 cases with an unknown connection.
⟦26⟧ On December 30, 2019, there were 4 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 16 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 1 case with an unknown connection.
⟦27⟧ On December 31, 2019, there was 1 case with a vendor connection to HSM, 4 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, and 10 cases with a non-HSM connection.
⟦28⟧ In total, there were 30 cases with a vendor connection to HSM, 25 cases with a visitor connection to HSM, 113 cases with a non-HSM connection, and 6 cases with an unknown connection.
